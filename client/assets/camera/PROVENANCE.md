# Original mock optical scenes

`service-deck.png` and `relay-deck.png` were generated for this project with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-01. No game screenshot, original UI, logo, artwork or layout was supplied as a reference.

The specification was an invented physical orbital engineering compartment: electrical cabinet at left, pressure hatch at centre, light controller at right, cables, insulation, bolts, practical low illumination and camera grain. These are static prerendered mock scenes reused by the eight simulated camera nodes, not live video or recorded observations. The node identity, state and controls remain authoritative server data.
